## 📅 Worklog – Jiki Translatathon (31 July–2 August) 2026 – Italian Localisation

## Contribution by Daniela – Kernela Klees (London, UK)

### Overview

I participated in the Translatathon 2026 as part of the Italian localisation team for Jiki. 
My contribution focused on **editing, refining, and improving existing content**, ensuring high 
linguistic quality, consistency, and natural Italian phrasing. 
The initial drafts were already available, and my role was to elevate them through careful editorial 
work rather than producing direct translations myself.

Although the event took place from **31 July to 2 August**, I continued contributing until **23 August** 
to help finalise outstanding reviews and further improve overall quality.

### During this period, I worked on:

- **Editing and refining content** to improve clarity, tone, and naturalness
- **Carrying out consistency checks** across terminology, style, and formatting
- **Enhancing overall quality** for accuracy, coherence, and readability
- **Finalising pending** reviews and resolving issues flagged by the team
My work ensured that the Italian localisation was coherent, polished, and aligned with Jiki’s voice, strengthening the final user‑facing quality of the project. 

### What I Worked On

- **Editing and refining educational content** (lessons, explanations, exercises)

### Focus Areas

The Italian localisation achieved a strong level of clarity, coherence, and consistency. My contributions helped prepare the language for the final bulk‑translation phase, ensuring that the material was polished, reliable, and ready for wider deployment.
